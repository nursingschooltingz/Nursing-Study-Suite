# v19.0 release verification

Date: 2026-10-18. Release: v19.0. Prepared from the v18.6 tree at commit `5aae2ff`.

## Why

The user specified five locked-in requirements for v19.0:
1. Plain JavaScript function declarations (`text/babel` safe, without `export` keywords) for Point-ID parsing, dynamic schema construction, and deterministic KB auditing.
2. Dynamic `enum` schema generation (`buildKbFactItemSchema`) locking `pointAnchors` to exact Point IDs (`P1…Pn`) plus a `"NONE"` escape hatch, together with ready-to-run `SOURCE & ANCHOR RULES` injected via `kbBuildFocusBlock`.
3. Regex fix for dotted section numbers (`2.1`) and heading filters (`# Topic`, `**Topic**`, and bare `Label:` lines) in `parseAndNumberPoints` while preserving clinical numbers (`12-lead ECG`, `2 L/min O2`).
4. Step 6 deterministic KB Audit (`auditKbFacts`) with duplicate-tag deduplication (`['P1', 'P1']` counted once), partial invalid array flagging (`['P1', 'P99']`), `0`-fact uncovered Point detection, and dynamic `> 2× median` bleed detection (`Math.max(medianCount * 2, 1)`), surfaced in the Knowledge Base tab UI.
5. Step 7 Anki 4-category workflow covering full LATTE scope (Clinical Discriminators, Exact Numeric Parameters, Direct Clinical Associations, and Nursing Interventions & Patient Education — LATTE T & E), front-anchored clozes, closed sets vs. open symptom lists, strict source grounding for priority sequences and numeric thresholds, and stripping `[P#]` / `[NONE]` tags from Anki fact packets and parsed cards.

## Change

- **Plain-JS (`text/babel`-safe) Point-ID helpers and built-in harness:**
  - `parseAndNumberPoints(rawPointsText)`
  - `formatPointsForPrompt(numberedPoints)`
  - `buildKbFactItemSchema(numberedPoints)`
  - `auditKbFacts(numberedPoints, kbFacts)`
  - `runPointAnchorHarness()`
  - `kbStripPointTags(text)` and `kbExtractPointAnchors(rawText, rawAnchors)`
- **Dynamic `SOURCE & ANCHOR RULES` (`KB_SOURCE_ANCHOR_RULES` in `kbBuildFocusBlock`):**
  - Formats user-supplied Points as `P1…Pn` via `formatPointsForPrompt(parseAndNumberPoints(points))` and appends `KB_SOURCE_ANCHOR_RULES` when focus inputs are active.
- **Step 6 KB Persistence & Point-to-Fact Audit Panel:**
  - `mergeLatteParts` and `kbNormalizeImported` extract any trailing `[P#]` / `[NONE]` tags from fact prose into `pointAnchors` and persist `numberedPoints` alongside the Knowledge Base across JSON export/import and IndexedDB restore.
  - `KnowledgeBaseBuilder` computes `pointAudit` and renders the Step 6 Point-to-Fact Audit panel (Uncovered Points = 0 facts, Unmapped/Invalid Facts = `[NONE]` or invalid ID, Max Facts & `> 2× Median` Bleed Suspects, and collapsible Point-to-Fact Audit Table). `KBFactList` renders `[P#]` badges and supports filtering by Point ID.
- **Step 7 Anki 4-Category Rules & `[P#]` Stripping:**
  - `kbForAnki` and `ankiParseCards` strip residual `[P#]` / `[NONE]` tags from fact text and parsed card `text` / `extra` while leaving untagged strings byte-identical.
  - `AnkiGenerator` formats numbered Points in `buildFocusBlock` and updates `kbAdapter` with the four exam-testable LATTE categories (including Nursing Interventions & Patient Education — LATTE T & E), front-anchored clozes, closed sets vs. open symptom lists, and strict source grounding for priorities and numeric thresholds.
- **Unchanged:**
  - All 11 frozen prompt constants and `prompt-baseline.json` are byte-identical.
