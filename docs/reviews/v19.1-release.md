# v19.1 release verification

Date: 2026-10-22. Release: v19.1. Prepared from the v19.0 tree at commit `6044618`.

## Why

Following the v19.0 release, a ten-phase production code review audited all seven tools, transport/intake utilities, and repository verification gates, identifying and remediating edge cases in clinical answer-keying, Point-ID and entity normalization, NCLEX allocation/audit/repair, Priority 2-stage progress and partial-tier synthesis, Anki audit-unit trimming, NCLEX Extractor/Converter parsing, PPTX/PDF/print/drawer lifecycle, and CLI/tooling gates.

## Change

- **Clinical answer-keying (`C1`):**
  - `n2aAnswerLabels`, `x2aResolveAnswer` (`x2aNorm`, `x2aTextHits`), and `i2aAnswerLabels` preserve decimal points between digits (`0.5 mg` vs. `5 mg`), require word boundaries on option text matches (`Productive cough` vs. `Nonproductive cough`), and require an explicit label separator or end-of-string on single-character label matching so `"2 mL"` and `"A client..."` are never misread as choice `2` or `A`.
- **Point-ID & entity normalization (`H1`–`H6`, `M2`, `M3`, `M12`):**
  - `parseAndNumberPoints` filters bare prefixes and bulleted headings; `kbStripPointTags`, `ankiParseCards` (including tags), and `ankiSourceSnapshot` strip `[P#]` / `[NONE]` tags without corrupting leading-dot decimals (`Administer .5 mg`) and normalize embedded newlines in tagged facts; `kbExtractPointAnchors` normalizes bracketed/comma anchors and drops `NONE` when paired with real `P#` IDs; `mergeLatteParts` and `normalizeKbEntities` strip `[P#]` tags from medication/diagnostic/scoring-tool fields into `pointAnchors`, bind chunk `sourcePointer` for derived facts, and preserve non-ASCII condition slugs across IndexedDB restore; `KnowledgeBaseBuilder` blocks builds while card photos remain untranscribed, isolates `importJSON` from source-chip removal aborts, and preserves persisted `numberedPoints` in Step 6 audit and `AnkiGenerator`.
- **Audit, repair, transport, converter & CLI gates (`H7`–`H11`, `M1`–`M15`, `L1`, `L2`, Phases 3–10):**
  - `NCLEXGenerator` skips post-audit repair after `QuotaStopError`, rejects no-op repairs, refreshes `citedAll` after repairs, pulls neighbor context from actual hit group objects when condition names collide, caps pre-filled focus facts at `perCondition`, and carries deferred focus picks into subsequent batches (`ngFocusPlan` also supports per-batch capacity limits via `batchCaps`).
  - `validateCaseStudy` resolves option-label Calculation answers; `caseShuffleQuestion` avoids cross-newline option-word corruption; `kbClassifyQuoteMiss` forwards decimal marks; `extractJSON` handles uppercase fences and multi-line-start JSON; `geminiRequest` maps numeric gRPC error codes and retains `bestPartial` across retries.
  - `paParseTiers` rejects rule-only sections and parses bold/label-italicized `Audit` footers, `paMergeTopics` merges `##` headings, `kbForPriority` and `kbForAnki` collapse embedded newlines, and `PriorityAnalyzer` scales Stage-1 harvest `0→50%` so progress never jumps backwards into Stage-2 synthesis, drops unparseable preamble fragments on failed parts when surviving parts parsed into tiers, and merges completed parts into `parsedTiers` when multi-part synthesis stops early on quota or cancel.
  - `ankiDedupeCards` unions `unresolvedFactIds` across exact-duplicate merges and `ankiAuditGroups` trims oversized chunk-wide support-fact payloads on unmapped-note units.
  - `nclexDropOptionRuns`, `n2aSplitStemOptions`, `n2aSplitBlocks`, `n2aStripSources`, `nclexAccumulate`, `nclexAdmitRecord`, `nclexRepairOptions`, `nclexMergePairs`, `NCLEXExtractor.runSplit`, `x2aBlocks`, `x2aParseExport`, `x2aBuildCard`, `x2aSplitRationale`, `i2aApplyAi`, `i2aBuildCards`, `i2aWorksheetMd`, and `i2aConvert` harden edge-case parsing and resume behavior.
  - `_zipNormalizePath` decodes URI-encoded OPC relationship targets; `markdownToPlainText` prevents bold span bridging across `**`; `useCappedLog` strictly caps log length at `cap`.
  - `pdfWalkPages`, `n2aReadPdfPages`, `NCLEXToAnki`, `FactInspectorDrawer`, `ResultsHead`, `Step`, `Disclose`, `CaseStudyGenerator`, `ConfigSidebar`, and the startup boot check guard post-await aborts (including during SHA-256 image hashing), unmount cancellation, transcript loading during active runs, PDF export cancellation, numeric zero props, empty condition lists, 2-stage Priority badge display, and missing CDN globals.
  - `verify-repo.js`, `tools/check-prompts.js`, and `tools/render-prompts.js` enforce strict CLI flag validation (`parseCliArgs`), `tools/visual-browser-tests.js` covers all 7 rail tools (`nclex2anki` included), `verify-repo.js` runs offline tooling and browser-fixture checks (`remediation-tooling-tests.js`) as a standard gate, `tools/v18-0-low-*.js` enforce start-anchor uniqueness, and `Prompts.md` documents all 16 prompt constants (including `KB_SOURCE_ANCHOR_RULES`).
- **Unchanged:**
  - All 11 frozen prompt constants and `prompt-baseline.json` are byte-identical.
