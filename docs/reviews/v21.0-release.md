# v21.0 release verification

Date: 2026-11-13. Release: v21.0. Prepared from the v20.3 tree at commit `c73ef64`.

## Why

v21.0 cuts over the Knowledge Base and all downstream study tools from the overlapping 6-bucket LATTE schema to the non-overlapping 5-bucket **P-LATE (Triple-E)** taxonomy (`Patho`, `Look`, `Analyze`, `Treat`, `Educate`), adds an opt-in RN Core vs. NP Mechanism Bridge layer (`clinicalLayer`, `plateSlot`, `KB_PLATE_BRIDGE_RULES`), injects validator-safe runtime 4-Pillar distractor & NGN Slope Red Flag synchronization directives (`NCLEX_PLATE_TRAP_RULES`, `CASE_SLOPE_SYNC_RULES`) alongside subtype-balanced `selectCaseFacts` quotas, updates the suite brand mark to `[P] P-LATE Study Suite` with HESI®/ATI® non-affiliation disclaimers, and fixes the `CaseStudyGenerator` lazy-mount Temporal Dead Zone `ReferenceError` on `caseStudy`.

## Change

- **5-bucket P-LATE (Triple-E) taxonomy, Pharmacology entity upgrade, & lossless legacy migration (`KB_FACT_BUCKETS`, `KB_ENTITY_FIELD_MAP`, `kbMigrateLegacyBucket`, `mergeLatteParts`, `kbNormalizeImported`, `normalizeKbEntities`, `kbBucketFacts`, `renderKBMarkdown`, `AnkiGenerator`, `GizmoImporter`, `NCLEXGenerator`, `CaseStudyGenerator`):**
  - Consolidates `Assess` and `Tests` overlap into `Patho`, `Look` (subjective history + physical exam), `Analyze` (labs, imaging, telemetry, diagnostics), `Treat` (`dependent` vs. `independent`), and `Educate` (`discharge` vs. `evaluate`), and routes standalone `medications` onto all 5 buckets with Pharm-native Study View headings.
  - Migrates saved/imported v15–v20 LATTE Knowledge Bases automatically (`BriefPatho -> Patho`, `Tests -> Analyze`, `Treatments -> Treat`, `Assess -> Look / Analyze / Treat / Educate` by clinical target) and updates hierarchical Anki/NCLEX tags (`Nursing::PLATE::*`, `PLATE::*`) while preserving legacy `Nursing::LATTE::*` compatibility.
- **RN Core vs. NP Mechanism Bridge firewall (`KB_SOURCE_ROLES`, `KB_PLATE_BRIDGE_RULES`, `capBridgeTier`, `kbCoercePlateFields`):**
  - Adds per-source Step 01 role pills (`rn`, `patho-bridge`, `exam-bridge`, `pharm-bridge`) across PDFs, PPTX decks, and card/transcript chips, merges cross-book acronym/full-name conditions while letting RN Core win on duplicate facts, caps non-safety graduate bridge facts at Tier 2 (`capBridgeTier`), renders collapsible NP Bridge `<details>` sections in Study View, stamps `Layer::RNCore` / `Layer::NPBridge` in Anki, and excludes `clinicalLayer === 'bridge'` facts by default across Priority, NCLEX Generator, and Case Studies until **Include NP Bridge facts** is enabled.
- **Approved frozen prompt updates + runtime 4-Pillar & Slope directives (`docs/history/PLATE-v21.0-frozen-prompts.diff`, `NCLEX_PLATE_TRAP_RULES`, `CASE_SLOPE_SYNC_RULES`, `selectCaseFacts`, `selectCaseFactsMulti`):**
  - Applies user-approved updates to 5 frozen constants (`KB_EXTRACTION_PROMPT`, `KB_VERIFY_PROMPT`, `ANKI_MASTER_PROMPT`, `NCLEX_GEN_PROMPT`, `NCLEX_DISTRACTOR_RULES`), leaving the remaining 6 frozen constants byte-identical.
  - Injects non-frozen `NCLEX_PLATE_TRAP_RULES` into `NCLEXGenerator` batch packets and `CASE_SLOPE_SYNC_RULES` into `caseBuildPrompt` (keeping plain-text `PART 1–PART 4` worksheet parsing, `(Source: C#)` citations, and qualitative `stage.narrative` rules intact), and rebalances `selectCaseFacts` (`3/12/12/12/8 = 47`) and `selectCaseFactsMulti` (`4/12/12/12/8 = 48`) with `Treat` and `Educate` subtype sub-quotas.
- **Branding, legal notices & bug fixes (`App`, `CaseStudyGenerator`, `README.md`):**
  - Updates the top-left brand mark in-place to `[P] P-LATE Study Suite`, adds **HESI®** (Elsevier Inc.) and **ATI®** (Assessment Technologies Institute, LLC) non-affiliation notices in `.rail-foot` and `README.md`, and moves `caseStudyRef.current = caseStudy` below `const [caseStudy, setCaseStudy] = useState(null)` to eliminate the Case Studies tab render crash.
