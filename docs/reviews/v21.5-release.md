## Scope Summary

1. **Bedside Functional Exams & Clinical Scales in `Look` (`KB_EXTRACTION_PROMPT`, `ANKI_MASTER_PROMPT`, `kbCoerceEntityBucket`, `normalizeKbEntities`)**
   - Clarifies `Look vs. Analyze` in `KB_EXTRACTION_PROMPT` and `ANKI_MASTER_PROMPT` (user-approved diff recorded in `docs/history/PLATE-v21.5-look-analyze-scales.diff`) so bedside sensory/cranial-nerve/neurovascular exams (`Snellen`, `Romberg`, `monofilament`, `capillary refill`, `6 Ps`) and bedside clinical risk/severity scales (`Braden`, `Morse`, `GCS`, `NRS/Wong-Baker`) route deterministically into `Look`.
   - Routes `scale|score|screen|physical` buckets and standalone `scoringTools` fallback/flat-field derivation into `Look` while keeping `medications` fallback in `Treat` and `diagnostics` fallback in `Analyze`.

2. **Retention-First Concept Exemplar Anchors, Taxonomic Sets, & Pre-Procedure Safety Rescue (`KB_SOURCE_ANCHOR_RULES`, `KB_CONCEPT_CURRICULUM_RULES`, `KB_EXAM_TRAP_RULES`)**
   - Narrows `KB_SOURCE_ANCHOR_RULES` Rule 5 strictly to non-clinical filler while keeping demographic/age risk cutoffs, screening thresholds, and specific infection-control/hand-hygiene protocols.
   - Replaces lossy exemplar pruning in `KB_CONCEPT_CURRICULUM_RULES` Rule 3 with retention-first delta guidance (`MINIMUM EXEMPLAR ANCHOR` + single-chunk Part B Pass-2 visibility guard) and expands Rule 4 + `KB_EXAM_TRAP_RULES` Rule 1 to tag parallel taxonomic/classification sets (`Types I–IV hypersensitivity`, `IgG–IgD`, `innate vs. active/passive immunity`, `COX-1 vs. COX-2`, `A-delta vs. C pain fibers`, `Stage 1–4/unstageable/DTPI`).
   - Rescues mandatory pre-/post-procedure diagnostic nursing safety checks (`contrast eGFR/metformin`, `NPO/gag return`, `MRI metal screen`, `post-biopsy neurovascular checks`) into `Treat` under `KB_EXAM_TRAP_RULES` Rule 3.

3. **Legacy Medication `Assess` Toxicity Routing, Bridge Differentiator Protection, & Anki `ZERO-SKIP` Packing (`kbResolveLegacyAssessBucket`, `kbMigrateLegacyBucket`, `kbBucketFacts`, `kbCoercePlateFields`, `AnkiGenerator`)**
   - Keeps observable bedside medication toxicities (`tinnitus`, `visual halos`, `angioedema`, `respiratory depression`, `sedation`, `orthostatic hypotension`, `bleeding`) in `Look` rather than forcing 100% of legacy medication `Assess` facts into `Analyze`, and gives pre-procedure hold/safety rules precedence into `Treat`.
   - Protects `conceptScope === 'differentiator'` and active contrast/safety `trapRole` bridge facts at Tier 1 when `capBridgeTier` is enabled.
   - Replaces `kbAdapter` card-layer filtering with `ZERO-SKIP` conversion and non-lossy `c1`–`c3` multi-cloze packing, relabels `#anki-scope-mode` into `Full Coverage` vs. `Partial Cram` modes, and displays a live `role="status"` warning banner with exact excluded entry/fact counts whenever a partial scope or text filter is active.
