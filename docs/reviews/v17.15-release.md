# v17.15 release verification

Date: 2026-10-13. Release: v17.15. Prepared from the v17.14 tree at commit `c82ce02`.

## Why

Finding C1 of the [v17.14 production review](production-review-2026-10-13.md): a "part 4" mention before the real PART 4 heading made `ngSplitParts` start PART 4 early, so PART 4 contained PART 3. `ngShuffleWorksheetOptions` spliced PART 3 and then PART 4 with `ngSpliceSection`, which returns the document unchanged when it cannot find the old text. The choices were shuffled and the ANSWER and Why lines were not, with no validation error. The repair path had used the same splice since v15.8.

Triggers reproduced on v17.14:
- PART 2 restating the batch rule the app sends (line 10525 of v17.14);
- a mid-sentence "Part 4" such as the prompt's own 'Part 4 "Why" lines';
- a PART 1 anchor naming "Resp Care Part 4.pdf".

## Change

- `ngSplitParts`: a heading must start its line, after any non-word characters (Markdown, emphasis, rules), and must not be followed by "and", "or", "&", ",", "/" or a dash and digit, so "PART 3 and PART 4 must…" and "PART 1-2" are not headings. If the line-start match finds nothing, the v17.14 match is used unchanged.
- `ngSpliceParts(doc,P,np3,np4)`: locates both sections in the original text and replaces them in one pass. It returns null when either is missing or they overlap.
- Callers:
  - `ngShuffleWorksheetOptions`: on null, every item keeps its written order and is reported as kept.
  - `ngLetterNumberedOptions`: on null, the batch is returned as written, so it fails validation visibly, as before.
  - The repair: on null, the repair is refused ("could not locate PART 3 and PART 4 separately") and the worksheet is untouched.
- **Step 2: one letter-reference check** (findings H3, M8, M10, M11).
  - `itemRefersByLetter(text,labels)` sits before `ngShuffleWorksheetOptions` and is used by it and by `caseShuffleQuestion`. It replaces the v17.12 local `refersByLetter`/`LISTED` and the v17.1 case check.
  - **Default.** A capital letter that matches a label is a reference unless it is:
    - a sentence-opening article A not followed by a verb such as "is" or "would";
    - preceded by a clinical noun (vitamin, hepatitis, type, group, class, factor, stage, grade, schedule, phase, step, part, level, plan, lead, blood, strep);
    - a C or F after a number or a degree sign;
    - hyphenated (D-dimer), an abbreviated organism ("C. difficile"), or A&O/I&O.
  - **Also references:** position words; "all/none/both/neither/either/any of these/them/those/the above…"; lists, ranges and parenthesised labels; and lowercase labels after "option" or "choice". A singular "option/choice/answer B" is still relabelled.
  - **Misnumbered batches.** `ngShuffleWorksheetOptions` returns `{misnumbered:true}` and shuffles nothing unless both the PART 3 numbers and the PART 4 ANSWER numbers run 1..n. The generator logs it.
  - **Case SATA keys.** `caseShuffleQuestion` sorts the mapped SATA key.
- **Step 3** (findings H2, H1, H9, H4, H5).
  - **H2 — ordering rewrite.** `ngCanonicalOrderingAnswers` declines step-line evidence when the lines carry rank numbers that are not 1..n in order, or when they only repeat the presented order. This is reviewer F's tested guard. Every shipped v17.8 shape is still rewritten.
  - **H1 — question splitting.** `n2aSplitBlocks` also starts a question at a numbered line after a blank line. `n2aConvertText` compares the questions found with the export's "N questions generated/extracted" line and marks every card NeedsReview on a mismatch.
  - **H9 — image continuations.** `i2aAssemble` tracks the item each image ended with. A continuation at the top of the next image merges into that answer-key entry or that question. Merging into a question not marked `cut_off` adds a review flag.
  - **H4 — choice runs.** `nclexStartsRun` and a trim in `nclexDropOptionRuns` stop a choice run before the question number the sequence expects next, when a new choice run follows. This is reviewer D's tested fix.
  - **H5 — repeated numbers.**
    - `nclexSliceByQNum` appends a repeated number's slice to the item before it and records the repeats.
    - `nclexPairQA` reports them.
    - `runSplit` logs them and sets `track.review`, so `nclexRunSummary` reports Incomplete.
    - Pairing chapters segment by segment is not done; it is an owner decision.
- **Step 4** (findings H6, H7, H8, H10, H11).
  - **H6 — Study view headings.** `renderKBMarkdown` prints dependent treatments only under Dependent / Collaborative Orders. Other subtypes go under "Other Treatments (not classified as independent or dependent)", and unstaged Look facts under "Other Findings (not staged as early or late)".
  - **H7 — keyed options.** `validateCaseStudy` runs `caseAuditTextValues` on each keyed option (not Calculation) against its rationale's citations and reports the findings as warnings.
  - **H8 — unread units.** `caseAuditDatumValues` warns when a non-neutral datum value has a digit but yields no token and no unsupported entry. Labels age, room, bed, time, date and day are exempt.
  - **H10 — decimal quotes.** `kbNormForMatch(s,true)` and `kbDehyphNormForMatch(s,true)` produce a same-length twin in which a decimal point between digits is "~". `kbQuoteNumericBoundedMatch` treats it as a digit neighbour; it ignores the twin unless the lengths agree. The build passes the twin for both quote passes. Matching still uses the unchanged normalization.
  - **H11 — build-time KB check.** `mergeLatteParts` leaves out a fact with an unknown category or non-text subtype/factType and lists it on a non-enumerable `dropped` property; nothing is saved. A condition with no ASCII slug gets id "condition". The build logs the left-out facts and adds them to its warnings.
- **Step 5** (findings M17, M3, M14, M15, M7).
  - **M17 — Anki deck.** `AnkiGenerator.run` no longer clears cards, ledger, batch, source check, inspector, paging or edit block at the start; these are reset immediately before the new batch publishes. A failure or cancel logs that the previous deck is unchanged. Review decisions follow `batch`, so they survive too.
  - **M3 — halting.**
    - `transcribeCards` breaks out of the run loop on `geminiHaltsBatch(e)`, publishes the current card (failed, or an incomplete comparison), and throws a "Transcription stopped at/after …" error.
    - The KB build sets `auditHalt` on the first halting audit failure; later chunks record audit status `skipped` and send no audit request. The warning "omission audit stopped after …" joins the build warnings.
  - **M14 — model-written answers.**
    - `runSplit` records the unmatched question numbers, adds a review note (Incomplete), and sets `answer_unmatched` on records for those numbers; `nclexAdmitRecord` keeps the flag.
    - The card shows a warning.
    - The .txt and .md/Copy/PDF answer keys append a note to the rationale. Kept inside the rationale paragraph, it leaves the answer line NCLEX to Anki reads unchanged.
  - **M15 — overlapping copies.** `nclexAccumulate` fills text fields missing from the first copy of a question from a later copy and never overwrites one. Exports print "(not extracted)" for a missing answer or rationale.
  - **M7 — invalid-case export.** It saves `'VALIDATION FAILED\n'+invalidCaseRaw` with `downloadBlob`, without `markdownToPlainText`.
- **Fixed areas.** No prompt, profile, existing warning tier or export format changed. `DECISIONS.md` records the decimal-quote rule, the build-save rule and the two new case warnings.
