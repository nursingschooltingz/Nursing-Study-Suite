# v18.6 release verification

Date: 2026-10-15. Release: v18.6. Prepared from the v18.5 tree at commit `7771ed2`; the change itself is commit `882c5ce`.

## Why

After v18.5 the user asked whether the NCLEX Generator's Outcomes, Points and Additional Context boxes are still respected. They reached the model as prompt text (`buildFocusBlock`, appended to every batch prompt), but the allocator never read them, before or after v18.5.

A batch may write only from the facts allocated to it, because the citation check rejects facts not supplied to that batch. So a Point whose facts no batch received could not get a question, even though the prompt says every listed Point should get one. The condition filter was the only real way to steer allocation.

A matching rule and its false-match risk were shown to the user first, measured with a scratch prototype. The user approved it.

## Change

- **`ngFocusItems`.** One item per line or `;`-separated part of the Points and Outcomes boxes, with bullets and list numbers removed.
- **`ngFocusMatch`.**
  - **Name match.** An item names a group when the group's name or an alias appears in it as whole words. Case and a plural s are ignored, but an all-capitals alias (MH, ALL, GCS) matches only in capitals. A name inside a longer name the item also contains is ignored.
  - **Word match (Points only).** A fact outside the named groups matches when it holds more than half of the item's words and one of them is rare in the pool (in at most 2% of its facts, or 5).
  - **Keep.** Only the facts with the most of the item's words are kept, named-group facts first, then pool order.
- **`ngFocusPlan`.** Item k (Points, then Outcomes) gets up to facts-per-question facts no earlier item took, placed in batch (k mod batches)+1. All items together take at most half of a run's target facts (`floor(targetCount·factsPerQ/2)`). The per-item quota shrinks to fit, to no less than 1.
- **`ngAllocateBatch(..., {first})`.**
  - **First.** The batch takes its picked facts first.
  - **Group limit.** Picks count toward their group's 4-per-group limit in the walk's first round.
  - **Second round.** A first round blocked by that credit gets the second round the walk would have had.
  - **The rest.** The v18.5 walk from `ngBatchStart` fills the rest of the batch.
- **`ngBuildFactPool`.** Pool groups carry their aliases.
- **Run log.** One line says how many Points and Outcomes matched and names up to five Points that matched nothing. Each batch's line says how many of its targets were picked for the Points and Outcomes.
- **Unchanged:**
  - every prompt, including the `buildFocusBlock` wording;
  - Additional Context (prompt only);
  - the condition filter;
  - per-run disjointness and the reserve-before-call rule;
  - the legacy whole-packet mode.

## Verification

- `node verify-repo.js`: all gates pass, **3,854 assertions (24 new)** in `tools/v18-6-focus-allocation-tests.js`.
- **Mutation check.** Thirteen mutations each fail the module:
  - removing the more-than-half rule, the rare-word rule, the longer-name rule, the capitals rule, the most-words filter or the named-first tiebreak;
  - removing the first-round credit or the second round;
  - skipping the picks, placing every item in batch 1, or dropping the cap;
  - dropping `first` at the call site or aliases from the pool.
- **Updated assertions.** The call-site pins in `tools/v18-0-low-cases-tests.js` and `tools/v18-5-allocation-tests.js`; version literals 18.6.
- **Prompt bytes.** All 11 frozen hashes are unchanged; `Prompts.md` was regenerated.
- **SRI.** The eight SRI-pinned scripts and both PDF worker copies were re-hashed against their CDNs and match.
- **Exam 1 KB, dry allocation** with the shipped functions (931 facts in 145 groups; no API call; the KB was read locally and is not in the repository).
  - **Method.** Twenty realistic Points written for that exam before measuring. For each, the facts that could ground a question on it were judged by hand. A Point counts when at least one allocated target fact is among them.

| Run | Points with an allocated fact (v18.5 → v18.6) | Groups reached |
|---|---|---|
| 20 questions | 5 → 19 | 18 → 28 |
| 25 questions | 9 → 20 | 23 → 33 |
| 50 questions (default) | 12 → 20 | 48 → 56 |
| 100 questions | 17 → 20 | 96 → 96 |

Notes on the table:
- **Picks.** In the default run 19 of the 20 Points got picks (48 facts, all on-topic) and every pick was allocated. The twentieth, *Cushing's triad / increased ICP*, matched nothing and the log names it; the walk happened to reach its facts.
- **Group counts.** Groups are counted by index. v18.5's figure of 45 counted names, so same-named entries counted once.

- **False-match probe**, 17 generic or off-KB Points:
  - **No match, as intended (10).** For example *Delegation to UAP*, *Therapeutic communication*, *Discharge teaching*, *Lab values*.
  - **On-topic (4).** *Seizure precautions*, *Older adult considerations*, *Normal vital signs*, *Pain management*.
  - **Weak or wrong (3).** *Fall risk*, *Safety*, *Blood transfusion reactions*.
  - **Cost.** One to three real KB facts take walk slots, still cited normally.
  - **Earlier draft.** A draft rule that accepted one rare shared word was much worse (*Seizure precautions* picked neutropenic precautions) and was not shipped.

## Not measured

- No live NCLEX run.
- Whether the model writes a question on a Point's picked facts: a batch has about 30 target facts for 10 questions, so a picked fact is not a promised question.
- A line naming two topics (*Cushing's triad / increased ICP*) can match nothing; splitting it into two lines is the workaround.
