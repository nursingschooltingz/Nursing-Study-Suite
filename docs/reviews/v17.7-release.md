# v17.7 release verification

Date: 2026-09-30. Release: v17.7. Prepared from the v17.5 tree at commit `eb96794`.

## Why

A Priority run on the user's Exam 1 Knowledge Base (62 conditions, 650 facts: 463 source-tier 1, 187 source-tier 2) hit MAX_TOKENS in synthesis. Its usage was input 39,345 · thinking 62,914 · output 2,618 · ceiling 65,536, and the guide stopped after two topics. The harvest itself completed. Thinking and output share `maxOutputTokens`, and the one synthesis request carried the whole inventory.

## Change

`paSynthGroups` packs harvest extractions into parts of up to `PA_SYNTH_PART_CHARS` (40,000 characters). Each part is synthesized with the unchanged `paBuildSynthPrompt`, and `paMergeSynthesis` rebuilds one guide that `paParseTiers` reads unchanged. Part failures follow the harvest's rule: `geminiHaltsBatch` stops the run, and other failures cost only their own part. `paResultNotice` names truncated parts and discloses the split. At or under the limit, the request, progress item, metadata and failure path are the same as in v17.5.

## Follow-up from the first real run

The user ran the first build on the Exam 1 Knowledge Base. It returned 5 parts with no truncation, but the guide had an "untitled" topic and repeated topics (Hypermagnesemia twice in a row). A replay of the shipped chunker on that Knowledge Base, printing counts only, found the cause in the prose chunker:

- **Headingless chunks:** 18 of 19 chunks began with about 1,500 characters of overlap copied from the previous chunk. That text had no condition heading and usually started mid-fact. The harvest prompt files headingless items under `[§ untitled]`.
- **Double ranking:** 109 of 650 facts sat in two chunks and were ranked twice when those chunks fell in different parts.

`paChunkText` now builds chunks from whole fact lines. Every fact sits under its own condition heading. The overlap repeats facts only inside a condition that continues into the next chunk, capped at a quarter of a chunk. The replay now gives 16 chunks, none with a fact before a heading, every fact line whole, and 5 repeated facts (all inside Cellular Regulation Alterations, the one condition larger than a chunk). `paChunkJoins` and `paSynthGroups` keep a continuing condition in one part. `paMergeTopics` combines same-heading `###` topics across parts and keeps a word-for-word repeated item once.

## NCLEX Generator follow-up

The user sent a 154-question generated worksheet PDF. Its text was extracted with the app's own PDF.js reader on a loopback-only scratch server. In the second half, Q102's six choices, numbered "1." to "6.", printed as questions 103–108, the key read "ANSWER: 1, 2, 3", and later question numbers drifted from their key entries. Each batch's last answer also carried the prompt's FINAL VERIFY list (`Q1: topic=… gate=…`).

- **Parser.** On a batch shaped like that, the shipped parser split the numbered choices into questions, and the validator reported 8 errors.
- **Printed PDF.** Rendering the same questions through the app's Markdown pipeline showed the choices as items 103–105 and the next question as 106. With `ngMdLiteralNumbers` the output reads 101, 102, 103, with the choices inside 102.
- **Validation banner.** The user's PDF carried no FAILED VALIDATION stamp, so that run's raw output probably differed from the reproduced shapes. The raw text was not available.

Changes: `ngLetterNumberedOptions` (relabel with the key, only when unambiguous), `ngMdLiteralNumbers` with the matching unescape in `markdownToPlainText`, and the PART 4 end in `ngSplitParts`.
