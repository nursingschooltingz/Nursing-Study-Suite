## Scope Summary

1. **NGN Case Study Authoring Contract & Legacy Byte-Identity (`CASE_NGN_TYPE_RULES`, `caseBuildPrompt`, `caseFactSufficiency`)**
   - Adds non-frozen `CASE_NGN_TYPE_RULES` and four toggleable Next Generation NCLEX (NGN) question types—**Bow-tie** (`Bowtie`), **Matrix / Grid** (`Matrix`), **Cloze Drop-Down** (`Cloze`), and **Serial Trend** (`Trend`)—to **06 · Clinical Case Study Generator**.
   - Preserves 100% byte-identical `caseBuildPrompt` output when all four NGN flags are disabled/omitted, maintains lossless `caseSplitSystemPrompt` prefix-cache splitting, and enforces `CASE_QUESTION_RULES` (4-Pillar distractor traps, anchor grounding, per-option/per-row rationales, completeness), `PRIORITY_ORDER_RULES`, `CASE_SLOPE_SYNC_RULES`, and the RN/NP Bridge firewall.
   - Adds optional warn-only NGN pool sufficiency advisories in `caseFactSufficiency`.

2. **Deterministic NGN Validation, Timing, Provenance, & Shuffling (`caseShapeIssues`, `caseValidateNgnQuestion`, `validateCaseStudy`, `validateStageTiming`, `caseAllFactIds`, `caseDifficultySignals`, `caseShuffleQuestion`)**
   - **`Bowtie`:** Enforces 3 option pools (`actions`: `4–5`, `condition`: `4`, `parameters`: `4–5`), exact `2 + 1 + 2 = 5` `correctAnswers`, per-token cited rationales, warn-tier keyed-token numeric checks, and independent per-pool Fisher–Yates shuffling.
   - **`Matrix`:** Enforces `2–4` columns × `3–6` rows (`single` or `multiple` mode), `"<rowId>:<colId>"` cell keys, 1 cited rationale per row, warn-tier numeric checks on affirmative rows while exempting `Contraindicated`/`Nonessential`/`Ineffective` trap rows, and row-order Fisher–Yates shuffling with clinical column order preserved.
   - **`Cloze`:** Enforces `2–4` contiguous `{{D1}}..{{D4}}` placeholders in `stem`, `3–5` options and `1` key per blank, per-option cited rationales, and independent per-blank Fisher–Yates shuffling.
   - **`Trend`:** Enforces `3–5` chronological timepoints × `2–5` parameter rows grounded via `direct`/`combined`/`inference`/`instantiated` thresholds, feeds validated row tokens into `presentedValues` and `factIds` into `validateStageTiming`, `caseDifficultySignals`, `caseAllFactIds`, and Fact Inspector provenance, validates MCQ (`4` options, `1` key) or SATA (`5–6` options, `2–4` keys) choices, and shuffles choices while keeping chronological timepoints intact.
   - **NEIA Gate Scope:** Keeps `caseIsGateEligible` strictly scoped to single-best-answer `MCQ` (`N/A` on all four NGN formats).

3. **Interactive `CaseRenderer` Widgets & Print / Booklet / Paired Exports (`CaseStudyGenerator`, `CaseRenderer`, `caseFormatAnswerSummary`, `caseToMarkdown`)**
   - Renders interactive 3-column Bow-tie buttons, accessible Matrix radio/checkbox tables, inline Cloze `<select>` menus, and serial Trend EHR flowsheets inside `CaseRenderer` while keeping `<script type="text/babel">` below the `500,000`-character ceiling (`496,926` chars).
   - Formats clean GFM tables, blanks, and human-readable Answer Keys across `.md`, PDF prints (`worksheetFactIds: false`), and multi-case Booklet/Paired Case Packets without leaking `[fact-*]` citations into student worksheets or blind `caseAuditPayload` context.
