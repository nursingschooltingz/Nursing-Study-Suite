# Nursing Study Suite v21.1 — Release Verification Record

Date: 2026-11-13

## Scope Summary

1. **Concept-Wide Sibling Exemplar P-LATE Matrix (`kbProjectConceptMatrix`, `kbFormatConceptMatrixText`, `renderKBMarkdown`, `KnowledgeBaseBuilder`)**
   - Automatically appends a deterministic, source-cited 5-column comparison table (`Exemplar | P — Concept Bridge | L — Hallmark Cue | A — Gold-Standard / Tie-Breaker | T — 1st Bedside / Key Rx | E — Slope / Red-Flag Trend`) to every Overarching Concept Overview (`entryRole === 'concept'`) with $\ge 2$ sibling exemplars in Study View (including single-entry browser view via `_kbGroups`), Markdown/PDF exports, and a 1-click **`📋 Copy Sibling P-LATE Matrix`** clipboard button.
   - Prioritizes RN-Core `differentiator`, `gold-standard`, `bedside-first`, and `trend-slope` / `pseudo-improvement` / `evaluate` facts while excluding `clinicalLayer: 'bridge'` facts and omitting the matrix on single-exemplar Concept Overviews.

2. **Standalone Medication & Diagnostic Auto-Linking (`selectCaseFacts`, `caseRenderFactPacket`, `ngAllocateBatch`)**
   - Pulls up to 4 high-priority `Analyze` and `Treat` facts (max 2 per entity: hold rules, antidotes, contraindications, therapeutic/critical lab ranges) from standalone `medication` and `diagnostic` entries linked by direct clinical mention or shared Giddens concept (excluding entities naming only a competing sibling exemplar) into single-condition/exemplar Case Studies (`[LINKED ENTITY: <Name> (<kind>)]`) and `NCLEXGenerator` batch `SUPPORTING CONTEXT`.

3. **Interrelated Concept Cascade Framing (`caseBuildPrompt`, `CaseStudyGenerator`)**
   - Adds **`Interrelated Concept Cascade`** (`mixMode: 'cascade'`) alongside `'comorbid'` and `'differential'` for multi-entry unfolding cases (`Stage 1` primary concept disruption → `Stage 2` secondary interrelated-concept complication → `Stage 3+` `[Pharm / Lab Hold Trap]` & trajectory evaluation), auto-enabling `caseIncludeRelated` when selected on a Giddens concept scope while keeping single-entry prompts byte-identical.

4. **One-Click Condition-to-Concept Linker in Step 6 (`auditKbConcepts`, `kbAssignConditionConcept`, `KnowledgeBaseBuilder`)**
   - Exposes `unlinkedConditions` alongside `orphanExemplars` in Step 6's **Concept Curriculum Audit (P-LATE)** without inflating `issueCount`, with 1-click `<select>` controls to assign unlinked or orphan conditions under an overarching Concept Overview, promote a condition to `◆ Concept Overview`, or unlink back to standalone directly in IndexedDB-persisted KB state (zero API calls).
