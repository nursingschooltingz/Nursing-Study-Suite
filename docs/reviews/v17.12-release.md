# v17.12 release verification

Date: 2026-10-09. Release: v17.12. Prepared from the v17.11 tree at commit `c955a30`.

## Why

The user reported that nearly every correct answer from the NCLEX Generator was A. The worksheet printed each choice where the model wrote it. `NCLEX_GEN_PROMPT` (frozen) forbids surface cues such as making the key the shortest option, but sets no answer position, and the model defaults to A. The case generator has shuffled its options since v17.1; the worksheet never did.

## Change

- `ngShuffleWorksheetOptions(raw, random)` runs on each batch after `ngLetterNumberedOptions` and `ngCanonicalOrderingAnswers`, before the batch is split, validated, audited or exported. Repairs, exports and the item audit therefore all see the shuffled order.
- Each lettered MCQ and SATA item (labels A, B, C… in order, a parseable ANSWER line, Why labels among the choices) is Fisher–Yates shuffled once. Choice lines move with their indented continuation lines. Blank separators stay in place.
- The ANSWER labels are rewritten (SATA in letter order). Each "Why X is" line is relabelled and the Why lines are printed correct first, then by letter. A singular "option/choice/answer X" in the stem, choices, rationales or strategy is relabelled with the choice it names. Source quotations and Tags are never rewritten.
- These keep the item in its original order, and the log names it:
  - any other letter or position reference ("A and C", "(B)", "options B and D", "Like B,", "the answer is B", a nonstandard "Why B is incorrect", "the latter", "first option");
  - a source quotation naming an option;
  - a layout that cannot be rearranged safely.
- An article "A", "hepatitis B", "vitamin D" and "D-dimer" are not treated as choice references.
- Ordering and Calculation items, PARTs 1-2 (working notes) and the DISTRIBUTION line are unchanged. A repaired item keeps the labels the repair returned, as before.

## Verification

- `node verify-repo.js`: all gates pass. 3,570 assertions (15 new), all eleven frozen prompt hashes unchanged, prompt documentation regenerated for the new filename only, LF/version checks and the full JSX Babel transform.
- New assertions, in `tools/worksheet-remediation-tests.js`, use synthetic worksheets only:
  - each item's correct choice texts and its choice-to-rationale pairs are identical before and after the shuffle;
  - `validateNCLEXWorksheet` returns identical findings before and after;
  - over 400 seeded runs the key landed on each of A–D between 70 and 130 times.
- CDN SRI pins are unchanged from v17.11 but were **not re-hashed**: the build environment's network policy returned 403 for cdnjs.cloudflare.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net. Recheck them before publishing.

## Not measured

No live Gemini generation was run. How often real items are kept as written (because their wording names a choice by letter) is unknown; the log reports it per batch.
